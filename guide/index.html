User Guide · v1.0.2

Getting the most out of Linger.

Everything you need to set up synced desktop lyrics, customize click-through behavior, fine-tune timing, and manage your 3-Mac license.

1. Installation & First Launch

  1. Download the Apple-notarized Universal installer from Download Linger (supports both Apple silicon and Intel Macs on macOS 14.0 Sonoma or later).
  2. Open Linger.dmg and drag Linger.app into your Applications folder. Keeping Linger inside /Applications is required by macOS for Launch at login and silent background updates.
  3. Launch Linger from Applications. A ♪ note icon appears in your macOS menu bar, and your 14-day full-featured free trial begins automatically—no account or credit card needed.

2. Connecting Music Players

  • Apple Music & Spotify: When you play a track, macOS may ask to allow Linger to control Spotify or Music. Approving this Automation permission lets Linger read sub-second playback progress directly. You can check or grant access anytime in Settings › General › Permissions.
  • NetEase Cloud Music: Works out of the box via macOS Now Playing with zero extra permissions.
  • One-Click Favorites (⌘L): Click the heart button next to the track title in the menu bar panel (or press ⌘L while the panel is open) to like the current song. For Apple Music tracks, Linger also marks the song as a Favorite directly in the Music app.

3. Window Placement, Click-Through & Visuals

  • Move & Resize: Drag the floating lyrics card anywhere across single or multi-display setups, and drag any corner to resize. Click Reset Position and Size in Settings › Appearance to return to default coordinates.
  • Desktop vs. On Top: Choose Desktop to pin lyrics peacefully above your wallpaper and behind active windows, or On Top to keep lyrics visible above full-window apps.
  • Lock Position (Click-Through): Enable Lock position in the menu bar panel or Settings so mouse clicks pass completely through the card to underlying windows, paired with Fade on hover when your pointer approaches.
  • XDR HDR & Aurora Intensity: On XDR MacBook Pro or Pro Display XDR screens, toggle Highlight current line in HDR and choose between Soft, Standard, or Vivid aurora shaders.

4. Timing Offsets, Alternate Lyrics & Offline Cache

  • Per-Song Timing Offset: In the menu bar panel, click − or + beside Lyrics timing to shift lyrics by ±0.5s, or hold Option while clicking for fine ±0.1s adjustments. Linger remembers your offset automatically for that specific song.
  • Manual Lyrics Search: Click Choose Lyrics in the menu bar panel to search alternate synced or translated versions from LRCLIB and NetEase Cloud Music and preview lines before applying.
  • Offline Cache: Synced lyrics, word timings, and album palettes are cached locally (up to 50 MB) so replayed tracks work offline. Clear the cache anytime in Settings › Lyrics › Storage without losing saved timing offsets.

6. License Activation, Moving Macs & Self-Service Refunds

  • One-Click Activation: After purchasing Linger Pro ($4.99 USD one-time) via Stripe Checkout, click Activate in Linger on the confirmation screen, or paste your LINGER-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX key in Settings › License.
  • Up to 3 Macs: One license activates up to 3 Macs simultaneously. Moving to a new Mac? Open Settings › License on an old Mac and click Deactivate This Mac to free the slot immediately.
  • 14-Day Instant Refund: Within 14 days of purchase, visit the Self-Service Refund portal, enter your license key and purchase email, and Stripe processes a full refund automatically. Need help? Email support@levicraft.app.

使用指南 · v1.0.2

余音 Linger 使用指南

从安装授权、点击穿透布局、逐字时间轴微调到激活码迁移，两分钟掌握全部细节。

1. 安装与首次启动

  1. 前往 下载页面 获取经过 Apple 官方公证的双架构安装包（原生支持 Apple 芯片与 Intel Mac，需 macOS 14.0 及以上）。
  2. 打开 Linger.dmg，将 Linger.app 拖入 应用程序（Applications） 文件夹（开机自启与后台静默更新均要求应用位于该目录）。
  3. 从“应用程序”启动余音，屏幕右上角菜单栏会出现 ♪ 图标，自动开启 14 天全功能免费试用（无需注册账号或绑卡）。

2. 连接音乐播放器

  • Apple Music 与 Spotify：首次播放时系统可能提示允许余音读取对应播放器，点击允许即可获得毫秒级进度同步（也可随时在「设置 › 通用 › 权限」中查看与开启）。
  • 网易云音乐：开箱即用，无需额外系统权限。
  • 一键红心喜欢（⌘L）：点击菜单栏面板歌名旁的爱心（或按 ⌘L）即可标记当前歌曲；播放 Apple Music 时还会同步标记为系统“音乐”App 中的喜爱歌曲。

3. 窗口层级、点击穿透与视觉调节

  • 自由拖拽与缩放：直接拖动悬浮歌词卡片换位置，拖四角调尺寸；在「设置 › 外观」点“重置位置和大小”可一键归位。
  • 桌面层 vs 最前：选择桌面可让歌词安静贴在壁纸上方、所有工作窗口下方；选择最前则始终置顶悬浮。
  • 锁定点击穿透：开启锁定位置后，鼠标点击会完全穿透悬浮窗直达底层软件，搭配鼠标悬停时变淡互不遮挡视线。
  • XDR HDR 与流光强度：在支持 XDR 的屏幕上可开启“HDR 高亮当前句”，并在柔和 / 标准 / 华丽三档流光强度间切换。

4. 单曲时间偏移、手动换词与离线缓存

  • 单曲时间偏移：在菜单栏面板“歌词时间”旁点按 − / + 可按 ±0.5 秒 调节，按住 Option 点击可按 ±0.1 秒 精细微调，每首歌自动独立记忆。
  • 手动搜索换词：点击菜单栏面板的换歌词，可同时搜索 LRCLIB 与网易云音乐的候选歌词并实时预览切换。
  • 离线缓存：听过的歌词与封面色板自动缓存在本地（上限 50 MB），断网也能照常显示；可在「设置 › 歌词 › 存储」一键清理缓存（不影响已保存的单曲偏移）。

5. 静默自动更新与许可管理

  • 无打扰静默更新：默认开启后台静默更新，自动下载并校验签名，在未播放空闲时或退出应用时无感完成替换；更新后可在设置左下角新版本动态（What’s new）查看分类更新日志，也可在「设置 › 通用」随时关闭自动更新。
  • 激活与换机迁移：在 Stripe 结账页 一次性支付 $4.99 美元后，点击页面上的“一键激活”按钮或在「设置 › 许可」粘贴激活码即可（最多同时激活 3 台 Mac，旧机点“在此 Mac 上停用”即可立即释放名额）。
  • 14 天自助全额退款：购买 14 天内可随时访问 自助退款页面 输入激活码与购买邮箱，系统将通过 Stripe 即时全额原路退回。售后联系邮箱：support@levicraft.app。
