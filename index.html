About Linger

A few things to know.

Is it free?

Completely free for now.

Where do the lyrics come from?

Lyrics come from third-party sources, LRCLIB and NetEase Cloud Music. Linger does not provide lyrics itself. Some lyrics may be missing or inaccurate.

What about privacy?

No account. “Share anonymous usage stats” is on by default: update checks send a random ID plus the app and macOS version so we can count active users. IP addresses aren’t stored, and you can turn it off in Settings › General. When looking up lyrics, the current song title, artist, album, and duration are sent to the lyrics sources.

Why not the Mac App Store?

Reading NetEase Cloud Music’s playback state requires capabilities that the App Store does not allow.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit Linger, then drag the app to the Trash.
