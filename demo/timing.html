  1. 选择有权使用的音频，填写曲名与节拍，粘贴歌词，每行一句，可写「原文 | 翻译」。
    Choose your licensed audio, enter its title and BPM, and paste one line at a time: original | translation.
  2. 点「载入歌词」，再播放。在下方打轴区按空格记录当前字的起点：中文按字，英文按词。
    Load lyrics and play. In the timing area, tap Space at each character or English word’s start.
  3. Enter 结束当前句并跳到下一句；最后一字也要按空格，再按 Enter 记录句尾。打错可以回退或清空重来。
    After tapping the last word, press Enter at the line’s end. Undo mistakes or reset and start again.
  4. 完成全部句子后导出 JSON，检查时间，再将对象加入 tracks.json 数组。
    Finish every line, export JSON, review the times, then add the object to tracks.json.
